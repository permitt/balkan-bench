# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

ML researchers and engineers evaluating or shipping language models for Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian, and Montenegrin (BCMS); regional NLP community members contributing datasets and models; international labs checking multilingual claims. Technical audience; they arrive wanting the table, not a pitch.

## Product Purpose

BalkanBench is an open, reproducible benchmark and leaderboard for language models across the BCMS languages. One canonical number per (model, task, language), every result traceable to the exact dataset, config, and seed that produced it. Success: it becomes the reference eval for the region and attracts community-submitted models and tracks.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, quiet, credible. Apple-adjacent restraint: real data is the hero, no hype. Regional pride expressed through substance (hand-adapted datasets, named contributors), not folklore.

## Anti-references

- The previous brutalist/editorial identity (mono tickers, fake terminals, signal-red chips) - retired deliberately.
- Generic AI-SaaS landing pages: gradient text, hero-metric templates, eyebrow kickers over every section.
- Crypto-leaderboard aesthetics: trophies, gold ranks, gamification.

## Design Principles

1. Real data is the hero - show the actual leaderboard, not screenshots or mockups.
2. Every claim auditable - numbers on the site must be derivable from published artifacts.
3. Calm confidence - spring motion and translucency in service of clarity, never spectacle.
4. The table is the product - the landing page frames it; it never competes with it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG-minded: dialog focus management, keyboard paths for all controls, prefers-reduced-motion/transparency/contrast honored at token level. Latin-script diacritics (š, ć, ž) must render correctly everywhere.
